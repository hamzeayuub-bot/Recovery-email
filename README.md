# How I Set Up a Recovery Email for My Gmail Account

Documentation of how I linked a recovery email to my Gmail account to protect access in case I lose my password or my account gets compromised.

## Why I Did This

- If I forget my password, the recovery email helps me get back in
- If my account gets hacked, the recovery email helps me recover it
- It adds an extra layer of security

## Steps I Took

### 1. Signed in to My Gmail Account

- Went to [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com)
- Signed in with my email and password

### 2. Went to "Security"

- On the left sidebar, clicked **Security**

### 3. Found "How you sign in to Google"

- Scrolled down to the **"How you sign in to Google"** section

### 4. Clicked "Recovery email"

- Found it under **"Ways we can verify it's you"**
- Clicked **Recovery email**

### 5. Added a Recovery Email

- Clicked **Add a recovery email**
- Entered the email I wanted to use (e.g., a work email or a secondary email)
- Clicked **Next**

### 6. Verified It

- Google sent a verification code to the new email
- Grabbed the code from my other email inbox
- Entered the code into Google
- Clicked **Verify**

### 7. Done

- The recovery email is now linked
- The new email now shows on the Security page

## Security Tips

- **Don't use** the same email (another Gmail is fine, but a work or secondary email is better)
- **Make sure** you always have access to the recovery email
- **Update** the recovery email if you ever lose access to the other one
- **Also add** a phone number for 2-step verification

## Result

Now if I forget my password, I can recover my account through my recovery email. This gives me extra protection against losing access to my account.
